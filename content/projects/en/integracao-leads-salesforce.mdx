## Context

The sales team received leads through several channels and entered each one into
Salesforce by hand. Beyond the time it cost, manual entry created two quiet
problems: leads sitting for hours before reaching the CRM, and the same contact
recorded twice by different people.

## How it works

I built the flow in n8n: a webhook receives the lead, the automation normalises
the data, queries Salesforce through its API to decide whether the contact is
new or already known, and routes it to the right owner. Google Sheets acts as a
logging layer — you can audit what went through the flow without opening the
CRM.

## Result

Manual entry left the process. Leads land in Salesforce already qualified and
routed, and the sales team spends its time on the conversation rather than on
data entry.
