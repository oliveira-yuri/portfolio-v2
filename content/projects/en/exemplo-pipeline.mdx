## Context

Sales reports were assembled by hand from a handful of spreadsheet exports, a
slow process prone to human error at every month-end close.

## Technical decisions

I chose a pipeline with explicit extract, validate, and load stages instead of
a single script, so that any failure would point exactly to where the data
stopped matching.

## Result

Monthly consolidation dropped from 3 days of manual work to a 40-minute
automated run, with an auditable log of each stage.
