## Context

Every new contact for the firm landed in the same WhatsApp inbox: an employment
case, a family-law question and someone who just wanted the address, all mixed
together. Somebody had to read everything before forwarding it, and urgent
matters waited behind simple questions.

## How it works

I built an agent in n8n connected to the WhatsApp API. It runs the opening
conversation with conditional logic: it identifies the practice area from the
answers, qualifies the contact, and only then forwards it. The triage is
identical regardless of the hour or of who happens to be available.

## Design decision

I chose explicit conditional logic over letting a model decide freely. In a
legal context, triage that picks the wrong area sends a person to the wrong
lawyer — and an explicit rule is auditable: you can point at exactly which
answer produced a given classification.

## Result

The lawyer receives each contact already classified and qualified. The queue
stopped being an undifferentiated list and started arriving sorted by area.
